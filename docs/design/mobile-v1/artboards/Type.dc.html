Scaling rules
UnitsSizes are points on iOS and dp on Android. React Native scales them by the system text size, and nothing in the scale sets allowFontScaling={false}.
WrappingAt 200% every style wraps and nothing truncates, except the single-line ListRow title. That title stays one line at 100% and wraps to two at 150% and above.
Fixed chromeTabBar labels (11 / 14, 500 and 700 when active) are the one exception and stop scaling at 1.3×. Each tab keeps its full name as its accessibility label.
FontsPlus Jakarta Sans and Inter are not in apps/mobile/src/assets yet. Until they are bundled, the app falls back to SF Pro on iOS and Roboto on Android.
