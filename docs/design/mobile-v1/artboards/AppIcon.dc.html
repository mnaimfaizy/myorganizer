MyOrganizer Mobile v1 · App icon

App icon for iOS and Android

The home-screen icon is the brand shield, not redrawn, centred on a plain plate. The shield keeps its gradient in every colour mode; only the plate changes. Every file both platforms ship is generated from this one drawing.

iOS appearancesOne 1024 × 1024 image per appearance, no transparency. iOS masks the corners and derives every size. Shown masked here; the files are square.
Light · AppIcon.pngPlate #F8FAFC, the light background. Shield #7C3AED to #0F766E, check #FFFFFF.
Dark · AppIcon-Dark.pngPlate #030711, the dark background. The shield and check do not change.
Tinted · AppIcon-Tinted.pngGreyscale for iOS to recolour. Plate #000000, shield #FFFFFF to #9CA3AF, check #000000.
